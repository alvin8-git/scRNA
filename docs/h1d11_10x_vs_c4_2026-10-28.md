## 3. Verdict

- **The major lineages agree.** Endoderm (18 vs 19–20 %), endothelial (5 vs 5 %) and amnion-like
  epithelium (2.7 vs 2.1 %) match closely. Total cardiac (CM + CCP) is 26 % on 10x vs 21–25 % on C4.
- **Cardiac splits differ.** 10x calls more mature CMs (10 % NPPA/MYL3/TNNC1 vs 4–6 %). It has no
  separate progenitor cluster: about 16 % of its cells cluster on intronic CM-gene reads. C4's CCP
  label likely covers some of the cells 10x splits into CM and intron-driven clusters. Settling
  this needs the 10x matrix scored with the same Yap rules.
- **The "Pluripotent" call in C4 at D11 is weak.** POU5F1 is detected in about 1 % of C4 D11 cells,
  and 10x has no pluripotent cluster. The 4–6 % C4 Pluripotent group at D11 deserves a second look.
- **C4 "Proliferating" is broader.** It is 13–14 % on C4 vs 5 % on 10x, because the curated label
  includes HAND1+ cycling mesenchyme, while Cell Ranger isolates only the strongest cycling cells.
- **10x has its own unclear fraction.** About 30 % of its cells sit in stress/histone,
  marker-less or lncRNA clusters, so 10x does not make the problem of uninterpretable cells go away.
  Some of this comes from counting introns.
- **Overall**, the C4 D11 composition is corroborated at lineage level. The CM-vs-CCP balance,
  Pluripotent and Proliferating proportions are platform- or label-sensitive and should be quoted
  with that caveat.
